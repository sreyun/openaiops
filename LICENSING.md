### What AGPL-3.0 means for you

- **Free for internal self-hosted use** — deploy on your own infrastructure, no fee, no host limit.
- **Source disclosure for network services** — if you modify the software and offer it as a network service, you must make your modified source available to users (AGPL §13).
- **Copyleft** — derivative works must carry the same license.

### When you need a commercial license

A **commercial license** is available if you need to:

1. **Distribute closed-source** — embed the software (or parts of it) into a proprietary product without open-sourcing your code.
2. **Offer a modified SaaS** — run a modified version as a network service without disclosing your changes under AGPL §13.
3. **Embed into a product** — bundle the platform inside a commercial appliance or managed service offering.

To inquire about commercial licensing, open an Issue on GitHub or contact us directly.
